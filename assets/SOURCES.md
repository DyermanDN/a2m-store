Close flat frontal orthographic photography, no perspective, evenly dim neutral light, detailed natural stone grain, premium architectural material. Overall dark espresso charcoal, not pure black, not excessively veined. NO objects, no bottles, no products, no pedestals, no lamps, no borders, no shelves, no lettering, no logos. This is a flat tileable wall and pedestal material texture, not a scene. Natural photographic realism, not illustration.
